# Krewdoo Preliminary Name Check

**Checked:** September 1, 2026  
**Scope:** Preliminary public-web and registry screening only; this is not legal clearance or an attorney opinion.

## Result

The exact coined term **Krewdoo** did not surface as an existing company, product, or registered mark in the broad searches performed. Verisign's authoritative `.com` RDAP endpoint returned `404` for `krewdoo.com`, which normally indicates that the domain was not registered at the time of the check.[1]

The name is memorable and supports the intended story: a **crew** of agents that can **do** and assemble things together. It is broader than a brick-specific brand and can extend to other modular creative domains.

## Material risks

| Similar mark or brand | Area | Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| **KREW** | A live/pending U.S. mark in International Class 28 for golf-related sporting goods | The shared `KREW` element warrants a professional clearance search if Krewdoo later sells toys or physical play products.[2] |
| **Kreedo** | Early-childhood education, play-based learning, educational materials, and learning apps | This is the closest commercial lookalike and overlaps with a possible children-and-learning audience. The spelling and pronunciation are not identical, but the overlap means Krewdoo should not claim legal clearance without counsel.[3] |

## Product decision

The owner explicitly selected **Krewdoo** for the WebMCP competition rebrand. The implementation may proceed as a product and submission name, with these safeguards:

1. Do not state that the mark is legally cleared, registered, or exclusive.
2. Keep the company positioning centered on agentic assembly and developer tooling, not preschool curriculum or physical toys.
3. Use **Krewdoo Assembly Lab** as the competition-facing product expression to strengthen distinctiveness.
4. Before paid marketing, merchandise, fundraising, or a trademark filing, commission a professional search covering confusingly similar marks in software, education, entertainment, and toys.
5. Confirm and register the chosen domain through a registrar; an RDAP `404` is a point-in-time availability signal, not a reservation.
